
## Paying-account arithmetic for Tier 2 (scenario only)

At an **assumed realized average of ₹999 per paying account per month**, using the illustrative 3× ARR scenario above:

| Illustrative value | Required ARR | Required MRR | Accounts at ₹999/month |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| ₹10 crore | ~₹3.33 crore | ~₹27.8 lakh | ~2,800 |
| ₹20 crore | ~₹6.67 crore | ~₹55.6 lakh | ~5,600 |

At an assumed ₹499 realized monthly amount, the equivalent is about 5,600 and 11,200 accounts. A straight-line 24-month path from zero to the ₹999 case implies about 117–233 net account additions/month before churn. These are arithmetic examples, not NOVA prices, forecasts, a valuation opinion or promises. Churn, discounts, failed payments, taxes, refunds, provider costs, support and concentration would raise the required gross customer count.
