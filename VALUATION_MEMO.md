# NOVA AI — Preliminary Valuation Memo (Evidence-Limited)

**As of:** 12 November 2026  
**Status:** Internal planning memo only. Not a formal valuation, investment advice, fairness opinion, offer, or sale recommendation.

## Executive conclusion

The requested Tier 1 ask of **₹15–20 lakh is not currently supported by the available evidence**. The project has a substantial codebase and automated verification, but the current PR is unmerged, the latest revision has not been deployed to Staging, production-provider use is not verified, and material security, privacy, licensing, legal and transferability work remains.

No customers, revenue, retention, active usage, buyer interest, or independent appraisal have been evidenced. Therefore no reliable market sale price can be stated today.

## Method 1 — Cost to replicate

A defensible replacement-cost estimate requires:
- a complete code and asset inventory;
- independent estimation of engineering, QA, security, design, DevOps and documentation hours;
- region-appropriate loaded hourly/day rates;
- separation of reusable open-source components from original work;
- adjustment for unfinished features, defects, technical debt and third-party dependencies.

Those inputs have not been independently measured. CI passing is quality evidence for specific test suites; it is not proof of production completeness or a billable replacement cost. **No ₹15–20 lakh replacement-cost claim is approved.**

## Method 2 — Comparable transactions

No verified transaction dataset with sufficiently similar products, deal terms, geography, IP scope, revenue, growth, liabilities and date has been established in this review. Asking prices, company valuations and headline acquisitions are not interchangeable with completed asset-sale prices.

**Comparable valuation: not determinable from current evidence.** Do not use a competitor's funding valuation as a comparable sale price.

## Method 3 — Revenue / ARR scenarios

Current verified revenue is **₹0 evidenced** for this memo. A revenue multiple cannot produce a current valuation without revenue, retention, gross margin, growth and quality-of-revenue data.

The following is arithmetic only, not a recommended market multiple or a forecast:

| Illustrative ARR multiple | ARR corresponding to ₹10 crore value | ARR corresponding to ₹20 crore value |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 2× | ₹5 crore | ₹10 crore |
| 3× | ₹3.33 crore | ₹6.67 crore |
| 5× | ₹2 crore | ₹4 crore |

The multiple must be justified by real comparable transactions and the company's growth, retention, margins, concentration, IP, compliance and risk profile. Do not treat these scenarios as a promise or valuation.

## Tier 1 — What is supportable today?

- ₹15–20 lakh: **not substantiated**.
- A lower exact asking price: **not recommended until independent cost-to-replicate and legal/IP diligence are complete**.
- Current saleability: **not buyer-ready** because staging, commercial rights, account deletion/retention, ownership chain, license/asset provenance, backup/restore, operational controls and legal policies remain open.

The most valuable next step is to complete and independently evidence the remaining diligence rather than publish an invented valuation.

## Tier 2 — What must exist before ₹10–20 crore is even assessable?

1. A real product used by a defined customer segment.
2. Paid, recurring revenue with documented invoices and bank/accounting reconciliation.
3. Retention, cohort, activation, conversion, churn and gross-margin evidence.
4. A repeatable acquisition channel and transparent customer acquisition cost.
5. Product-specific differentiation measured against competitors on the same tasks.
6. Clean IP chain of title, licenses, privacy compliance, security controls and transferable contracts.
7. Reliable operations, backups, incident response and customer support.
8. A qualified independent valuation using current comparable deals and company financials.

### Traction evidence the owner would need to generate

- Recruit a small, permission-based pilot group under responsible adult supervision.
- Obtain informed consent and avoid collecting sensitive or confidential data.
- Define one customer problem and success metric before each pilot.
- Record only genuine product usage and outcomes; never fabricate testimonials or usage.
- Validate willingness to pay through lawful, adult-supervised research; do not collect deposits or payments before legal, tax, privacy and billing readiness.
- Publish a monthly evidence dashboard with active users, retention, paid conversion, revenue, refunds, costs and uptime only after the metrics can be independently reproduced.

## Evidence limitations

This memo relies on the repository and CI evidence reviewed on 12 November 2026. It does not establish market demand, customer traction, a completed security audit, legal compliance, exclusive IP, or a buyer's willingness to pay. Update it only when new verifiable evidence exists.
