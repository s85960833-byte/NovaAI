# NOVA AI — Preliminary Valuation Memo (Evidence-Limited)

**As of:** 12 November 2026  
**Status:** Internal planning memo only. Not a formal valuation, investment advice, fairness opinion, offer or sale recommendation.

## Executive conclusion

The requested Tier 1 ask of **₹15–20 lakh is not currently supported as a fair asset value**. A preliminary replacement-labour model estimates that a fresh parity rebuild of the current scope could cost roughly ₹35–105 lakh in agency labour, but that model is not an independent estimate and does not establish what an acquirer would pay for an unfinished, rights-unverified asset.

No customers, revenue, retention, active usage, buyer interest or independent appraisal have been evidenced. Therefore no reliable market sale price can be stated today.

## Method 1 — Cost to replicate

See [COST_TO_REPLICATE.md](COST_TO_REPLICATE.md) for the itemized 1,500–2,280 hour scope model. The range applies a published India agency-rate band from Clutch (US$25–49/hour) and a dated 12 Nov 2026 USD/INR reference of ₹93.68, producing gross labour parity of about **₹35.13–104.66 lakh**.

This is a directional planning estimate only. Hours are not measured or independently quoted. It covers the existing scope at roughly its current prototype stage, excludes further production remediation and recurring/vendor/legal/marketing costs, and does not adjust for open-source reuse or competitor alternatives. It is not a fair-value opinion and does not prove a buyer saves the full amount by acquiring NOVA.

## Method 2 — Comparable transactions

No verified transaction dataset with sufficiently similar products, deal terms, geography, IP scope, revenue, growth, liabilities and date has been established. Asking prices, company valuations, funding rounds and headline acquisitions are not interchangeable with completed asset-sale prices.

**Comparable valuation: not determinable from current evidence.** The Indian market review also found competing products publicly offering overlapping Hindi/Indian-language AI app creation and deployment. This makes a generic feature-list premium harder to support.

## Method 3 — Revenue / ARR scenarios

Current verified revenue is **₹0 evidenced** for this memo. A revenue multiple cannot produce a current valuation without revenue, retention, gross margin, growth and quality-of-revenue data.

The following is arithmetic only, not a recommended market multiple or forecast:

| Illustrative ARR multiple | ARR for ₹10 crore value | ARR for ₹20 crore value |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 2× | ₹5 crore | ₹10 crore |
| 3× | ₹3.33 crore | ₹6.67 crore |
| 5× | ₹2 crore | ₹4 crore |

At the illustrative 3× ARR case and an **assumed realized average of ₹999 per paying account per month**, ₹10–20 crore would correspond to:
- roughly ₹27.8–55.6 lakh monthly recurring revenue;
- roughly 2,800–5,600 paying accounts before churn, discounts, failed payments, taxes, refunds, support, provider costs and concentration.

At ₹499/month realized revenue, the arithmetic is about 5,600–11,200 accounts. A straight-line 24-month path from zero to the ₹999 case is around 117–233 net account additions each month before churn. These are scenario calculations, not NOVA prices, a forecast, an approved target or a guarantee.

## Tier 1 — What is supportable today?

- ₹15–20 lakh: **not substantiated as a fair asset price**.
- Replacement labour model: ₹35–105 lakh gross, subject to the limitations above.
- Current saleability: **not buyer-ready**. Staging, provider commercial rights, account deletion/retention, ownership chain, media/source provenance, backup/restore, operational controls and legal policies remain open.
- A buyer may prefer existing Indian competitors or a fresh narrow build; therefore gross replacement cost is not the same as buyer willingness to pay.

## Tier 2 — What must exist before ₹10–20 crore is assessable?

1. A real product used by a defined customer segment.
2. Paid recurring revenue with invoices and accounting reconciliation.
3. Retention, cohort, activation, conversion, churn and gross-margin evidence.
4. A repeatable acquisition channel and transparent acquisition cost.
5. Product-specific differentiation measured against competitors on identical tasks.
6. Clean IP chain of title, licences, privacy compliance, security controls and transferable contracts.
7. Reliable operations, backups, incident response and customer support.
8. A qualified independent valuation using current comparable deals and company financials.

### Traction evidence that must be generated

- Recruit a small, permission-based pilot group under responsible adult supervision.
- Obtain informed consent and avoid sensitive/confidential data.
- Define one customer problem and success metric before any pilot.
- Record only genuine product usage and outcomes; never fabricate testimonials or metrics.
- Any paid activity, contracts, accounts or transaction discussions must be run by a responsible adult with qualified legal/tax review.
- Publish metrics only after first-party logs and financial records can be reconciled.

## Evidence limitations

This memo relies on repository, CI and public-source review as of 12 November 2026. It does not establish market demand, traction, completed security audit, legal compliance, exclusive IP or buyer willingness to pay.
