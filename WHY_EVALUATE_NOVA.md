## What the current codebase contains

NOVA is an India-oriented Hindi-English web workspace organised into:
- **NOVA Learn** — learning and study workflows.
- **NOVA Build** — digital creation workflows.
- **NOVA Agents** — assistant and agent workflows.

The repository also includes an early NOVA Business Intelligence preview with deterministic CSV analysis modules for expense review, client contribution, team-level tasks, risk heuristics and assumption-based Digital Twin scenarios.

## Why a partner might inspect it

A qualified software operator could evaluate whether the existing source, workspace UX, BI calculation module, test foundation and documentation can accelerate a narrower product they already understand. A buyer would still need to independently verify:
- clean ownership of all code, media, fonts, icons and third-party components;
- permitted commercial use and transferability of every provider/API;
- current-head Staging and physical-device behavior;
- production authentication, durable quotas, billing boundaries, account deletion and retention;
- backup/restore, monitoring, support and second-host portability;
- real user demand and a same-task competitive benchmark.
