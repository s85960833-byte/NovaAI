# NOVA AI — India AI App-Building Competitor Scan

**Review date:** 12 November 2026  
**Branch:** `fix/nova-home-learn-first-mobile-20261109`  
**Method:** Public vendor pages, app listings and product descriptions. Vendor claims are not independent performance tests. This is a desk scan, not legal or market-share research.

## Executive conclusion

NOVA must not claim that Hindi/Hinglish app prompting, AI app generation, live preview, source export, voice prompting, deployment, or an app-builder combined with an assistant is unique in India. Multiple India-oriented products publicly claim overlapping capabilities.

This scan does **not** prove that every listed product is available, reliable, secure, or commercially successful. It only establishes that the capabilities are publicly advertised and therefore cannot honestly be described as exclusive without a narrower, reproducible benchmark.

## What NOVA cannot claim as exclusive

- Hindi, Hinglish or multilingual app prompting.
- Voice-led app creation.
- Natural-language app generation or full-stack generation.
- Live preview, code export, custom domains or publishing.
- A combined assistant and app-builder.
- India-oriented pricing, UPI support or mobile-first access.
- AI agents for building or development tasks.

A competitor's public claim is enough to make an unqualified exclusivity claim unsafe. It is not proof that the competitor performs the feature well.

## Narrower differentiation hypotheses worth benchmarking

These are hypotheses only; none is approved as a unique market claim today.

1. **Evidence-linked transformation:** bind exact input-file hashes, transformation versions, validation results, and replayable tests to each exported artifact.
2. **Portable proof bundle:** provide a reproducible source export, dependency inventory, environment-variable names, deployment recipe, backup/restore instructions and test evidence in one package.
3. **Business decision trace:** connect each NOVA BI recommendation to tenant-scoped source metrics, approval history, and measured post-action outcome.
4. **Safe change-control workflow:** preview a plan, show a file diff, run tests, preserve a restore point, and require explicit approval before applying or publishing changes.

These ideas may still be available elsewhere. Before claiming a moat, compare actual end-to-end results against at least three competitors on identical tasks and publish reproducible measurements.

## Required benchmark

For each competitor and NOVA, use identical Hindi and English tasks. Record:

- Successful usable output rate.
- Time to first usable result.
- Human correction minutes.
- Automated test pass rate.
- Export reproducibility on a clean environment.
- Cost per successful result, including retries.
- Language fidelity and whether the final UI remains bilingual.
- Data ownership, portability, and deletion controls.

Do not publish superiority claims until tests are run on current versions and the raw evidence is retained.

## Buyer and valuation impact

This competitive scan reduces the credibility of a broad uniqueness/moat claim. It does not by itself determine asset value. A buyer would need evidence of superior execution, lower total cost, clean ownership, secure operation, reliable portability, and a working live product. No customer demand, revenue, market share, independent benchmark, or acquisition interest is established by this scan.
