# NOVA Privacy Notice — Draft for Owner and Legal Review

> **DRAFT — DO NOT PUBLISH AS A FINAL LEGAL NOTICE.** The operator's legal identity, postal address, support contact, retention schedule, age policy, subprocessors and final deletion process must be confirmed before customer onboarding.

**Product:** NOVA AI  
**Production URL:** https://novaai-4auo.onrender.com  
**Last technical review:** 8 November 2026

## 1. Information NOVA may process

- Account email and authentication/session information when a person creates or uses a NOVA account through Supabase Auth.
- Chat text, chat titles and memory only when a signed-in user explicitly enables optional account cloud sync. Sync is off by default. Image attachments are excluded from this sync. The user can disable future sync or delete the stored cloud backup from Account settings.
- Chat messages, prompts, files, image inputs, audio/transcription content or generated outputs as needed to provide a requested feature. Depending on the selected feature and route, this content may be processed by NOVA's backend and forwarded to configured AI, search, image or speech providers.
- Company Cloud preview data, including organization membership, dataset metadata, calculated aggregate analyses, assumptions/evidence, workflow proposals, approval/audit events and branding. Current Company Cloud flows process raw CSV rows in the browser; do not assume all future integrations do so.
- Basic technical request information processed by hosting, security and provider infrastructure.

## 2. Storage and service locations

The current infrastructure uses Render in Singapore and Supabase in Ireland. Some data may therefore be processed outside India. Provider services may operate in additional regions under their own terms and privacy notices.

## 3. User controls

- Chat history is stored locally in the browser by default.
- Optional account cloud sync is off by default and requires an explicit opt-in.
- Users may turn sync off and delete their cloud chat backup from Account settings. Turning sync off alone does not erase an existing backup.
- The app offers local chat backup export and restore.
- A complete self-service account deletion and published data-retention schedule are not yet implemented. Do not onboard paying customers until these controls and the support-based deletion procedure are approved and tested.

## 4. AI and third-party providers

AI outputs may be inaccurate. When a feature routes content to an external provider, that provider processes the content under its own privacy terms. Users should not submit confidential, regulated, financial, employee, student or other sensitive information during the current preview.

## 5. Security and limitations

NOVA uses server-side provider credentials and database row-level security for the new per-user workspace sync table. These technical controls do not constitute a security certification or a guarantee that all product features are suitable for sensitive data. Company Cloud remains a preview. Availability and provider quotas can vary.

## 6. Retention, deletion and requests

The final retention periods, account-deletion process, backup expiry and response times require owner approval and legal review. Until then, no fixed retention or deletion deadline is promised. The published notice must contain a monitored support email and the operator's legal identity and address.
