# NOVA AI — Buyer Map (Hypothesis Only)

**Review date:** 12 November 2026  
**Purpose:** Identify buyer categories that might reuse parts of NOVA if its IP, security, portability, product quality and commercial rights are independently cleared.  
**Important:** The companies below are illustrative examples of adjacent businesses, not confirmed prospects. No acquisition interest, contact, willingness to pay or relationship is claimed. The map is not a recommendation to contact anyone now.

## Summary

The more plausible no-customer asset buyers are likely to be smaller Indian software agencies, vertical SaaS vendors, managed service providers and digital-skills training providers that could reuse a working bilingual workspace or product accelerator. Large enterprises, cloud platforms and established AI vendors have extensive overlapping capabilities; for them, a generic app/workspace clone is unlikely to be attractive without a narrow, proven differentiator.

Strategic fit below means only how NOVA's current modules might align with the buyer category if completed. It is not a probability of purchase.

## Prioritization

1. **First research segment:** small-to-mid Indian software agencies, vertical SaaS vendors and managed-service providers. They may value a codebase as a starting point if it demonstrably saves engineering time and is easy to transfer.
2. **Second research segment:** digital-skills providers and coding bootcamps. They need a proven learning workflow and teacher controls, not just a general AI chat interface.
3. **Long-shot strategic categories:** large IT integrators, cloud platforms, major AI model providers and global consultancies. They already build overlapping products and are unlikely to acquire a broad, unfinished assistant without a strong differentiated asset.

## Evidence required before any buyer approach

- A current-head Staging demo with reproducible end-to-end tests and no known critical defects.
- Verified chain of title for code, contributors, video/audio, fonts, icons and all embedded assets.
- Commercially permitted provider contracts and clear data-processing terms.
- Authenticated multi-tenant controls, durable quotas, spend protection, audit trails, account deletion and tested backups.
- One narrow differentiator with head-to-head test results and permission-based user evidence.
- Independently reviewed cost-to-replicate estimate and a clear list of what is included/excluded in the asset.
