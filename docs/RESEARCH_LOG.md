# NOVA 2.0 Research Log

**Rule:** Official documentation is preferred. A source being read does not mean every implementation detail is settled. Recheck time-sensitive prices, rules and browser support before release.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-17 | Can Render Free host staging? What are the limits? | Free web services spin down after 15 minutes idle and can take about a minute to wake. Free Postgres expires after 30 days, has no backups, and is not suitable for production. | https://render.com/docs/free | Use only for synthetic staging; do not store durable or real-user records. |
| 2026-11-17 | What does Render currently charge? | Render lists Hobby workspace at $0 plus compute; free compute exists with limits. Paid web compute starts at listed monthly prices; paid resources are not approved. | https://render.com/pricing/ | Keep infrastructure on free resources only. Any required paid capability is a blocker; prefer hand-over or a safe degraded mode. |
| 2026-11-17 | What is the official Razorpay standard pricing? | Razorpay's public pricing page currently describes a 2% platform fee plus applicable GST for standard transactions; the provider page also describes different instrument-specific terms. This is not a final commercial/legal determination. | https://razorpay.com/pricing/ | Payment economics must include gateway charges and taxes; implementation in Phases 1–6 uses a mock provider only. |
| 2026-11-17 | Can Razorpay webhook signatures be verified? | Razorpay documentation describes the `X-Razorpay-Signature` header and HMAC-SHA256 verification over the raw request body. Old webhook secrets may be needed for retry verification after rotation. | https://razorpay.com/docs/webhooks/validate-test/ | Design webhook verification as a pure function over raw bytes; test invalid signatures and idempotency with fixtures only. No payment account or live webhook. |
| 2026-11-17 | What does the DPDP Act say? | The Act establishes India's digital personal-data framework. Commencement is by government notification and can differ by provision; the 2025 Rules were notified in November 2025 with staggered commencement dates. | https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/02/Digital-Personal-Data-Protection-Act-2023.pdf ; https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf | Build data minimisation, purpose notices, consent records, deletion workflows and child-data safeguards; obtain qualified adult/legal review before public handling. |
| 2026-11-17 | What are the official national consumer complaint contacts? | National Consumer Helpline lists 1915 and 1800 11 4000, with stated operating hours and online/WhatsApp options. Contact details can change and must be rechecked before publication. | https://consumerhelpline.gov.in/public/contact | Store official contacts with URL and last-verified date; do not let the model invent phone numbers. |
| 2026-11-17 | What is the official RTI Online scope? | RTI Online is for Central Ministries/Departments and listed Central Public Authorities. State authorities may require separate state processes. The portal FAQ describes the online form and payment of prescribed fee. | https://www.rtionline.gov.in/faq.php | Separate Central vs State flows; do not present one portal or one fee as universal. Exact fee/exception matrix still needs a focused verification pass. |
| 2026-11-17 | Where can scholarship records be checked? | The National Scholarship Portal publishes scheme listings and academic-year application windows; individual dates/status change during the year. | https://scholarships.gov.in/Students ; https://scholarships.gov.in/All-Scholarships | Scheme records must be checked against the official scheme page and date-stamped; no large unreviewed dataset. |
| 2026-11-17 | What are PWA install requirements? | Installability depends on browser/platform. Chromium browsers require a web app manifest with key fields/icons and a secure HTTPS context; service workers are useful for offline behaviour but are not universally an installability requirement. | https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/Progressive_web_apps/Guides/Making_PWAs_installable | Build manifest/icons and HTTPS staging; test Chrome Android and Samsung Internet separately. |
| 2026-11-17 | Can Web Speech recognition be assumed to work in all browsers? | No. MDN marks SpeechRecognition as limited availability; some implementations use a server-side recognition service, so offline recognition cannot be assumed. | https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/SpeechRecognition | Feature-detect; offer text/manual fallback; physical Hindi voice compatibility remains NOT VERIFIED. |
| 2026-11-17 | How should 120 Hz frame work be measured? | Chrome documents Long Animation Frames observation and DevTools trace integration. The Master Prompt's budgets remain: 8.3 ms/frame at 120 Hz, 11.1 ms at 90 Hz, 16.7 ms at 60 Hz; main-thread work target ≤4 ms/frame. | https://developer.chrome.com/docs/web-platform/long-animation-frames | Add performance instrumentation and emulated traces; do not claim true 120 FPS without a real 120 Hz device. |
| 2026-11-17 | What is OpenAI API data retention? | OpenAI's API data guide states API inputs/outputs are not used for training by default; abuse-monitoring retention is generally 30 days, with endpoint-specific application-state and eligible retention controls. | https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data | Use only approved providers and disclose data flows; do not upload real personal documents during build/testing. |
| 2026-11-17 | How does Gemini free vs paid data use differ? | Gemini API pricing page distinguishes free and paid tiers; it states free-tier content may be used to improve products, while paid-tier content is not used for product improvement. | https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing | Do not use free-tier Gemini for sensitive document workflows; provider selection requires privacy review and explicit approval. |
| 2026-11-17 | What are India's commercial SMS sender requirements? | TRAI's sender guidance requires registration as a Principal Entity, registered headers, content templates and consent templates for commercial communication. This does not establish the full OTP-specific classification or provider cost. | https://www.trai.gov.in/advice-to-senders | SMS OTP is not in initial build; do not enable SMS. Revisit classification and pricing with provider/legal review before launch. |
| 2026-11-17 | What remains unresearched or insufficiently verified? | National Cyber Crime Portal/1930 source, exact Central RTI fee and state-specific rules, OTP provider prices, current AI model price/Hindi OCR benchmarks, Groq and Anthropic retention details, Android Chrome/Samsung Internet Hindi recognition matrix, iOS install/push support, TWA policy, Supabase free limits/backups and current migration tooling. | See official-source follow-up tasks in `docs/BUILD_STATE.md` | Keep these items open; do not seed unverified helplines, scheme records or legal claims. |

| 2026-11-17 | Are new Supabase public tables automatically exposed to the Data API? | No. For new projects from 2026-10-30, new tables in public schema are no longer exposed automatically. Exposure and RLS are separate controls. | https://supabase.com/changelog?tags=data+apis ; https://supabase.com/docs/guides/api/securing-your-api | Explicitly grant only the minimum required table privileges and keep RLS enabled on every public table. | 2026-11-17 | Did Supabase change HTTP TLS requirements? | Supabase announced removal of four older CBC-mode TLS 1.2 cipher suites from project HTTP APIs from 2026-11-10 at the earliest; modern TLS 1.2/1.3 clients are expected to continue working. | https://supabase.com/changelog?types=breaking-change | Use maintained Node 22 and modern HTTPS clients; test Auth, Data API and Storage from staging. |
