# NOVA 2.0 Marketing Kit — Pre-release draft

**Status:** PENDING OWNER REVIEW · NOT APPROVED FOR PUBLICATION  
**Release boundary:** Staging preview only. Phase 7 is not authorised.  
**Product name:** NOVA — Darbar, Sakha and Diwan.  
**Audience:** Indian students and everyday users who need clear, bilingual help with study/work conversations and everyday paperwork.

## Positioning

**One-line promise:** A Hindi-first, bilingual assistant workspace that helps you understand, draft and organise everyday work — with clear limits and official-source checks.

**Short description (English):** NOVA brings a focused assistant (Sakha) and practical paperwork tools (Diwan) into one mobile-first workspace (Darbar). It is designed to make complex information easier to understand without pretending to replace official authorities or professional advice.

**Short description (Hindi):** NOVA में दरबार आपका मुख्य workspace है। सखा सामान्य सवालों और बातचीत में मदद करता है, जबकि दीवान रोज़मर्रा के कागज़ात और आवेदन के मसौदे समझने में मदद करता है। ज़रूरी जानकारी को आधिकारिक स्रोत से जाँचें।

## Feature messages

- **Darbar:** One simple home for the NOVA workspace.
- **Sakha:** Hindi/English chat preview with local conversation history and browser voice fallback. Current staging responses are mock-only.
- **Diwan:** Scam-awareness indicators, RTI/complaint draft templates, conservative document preview and deterministic scheme matching. The official scheme dataset is empty pending review.
- **Wallet:** A synthetic test ledger for engineering checks only. It is not a real wallet and cannot accept money.
- **Privacy:** Build/staging uses synthetic test data. Real provider calls, hosted sign-in, and real document processing are not enabled by default.

## 30-second demo script

1. Open Darbar and switch Hindi/English.
2. Open Sakha; send a harmless synthetic question and point out the visible MOCK label.
3. Open Diwan; run the scam-awareness example with a synthetic message.
4. Open RTI or complaint draft; show the editable template and official-process reminder.
5. Open Wallet; show that test credits are mock-only and no payment is collected.
6. Close with: “NOVA helps prepare and understand. Always verify official information before acting.”

## Store / landing page FAQ

**Is NOVA live?** No. This is a private staging preview, not the production app.  
**Does it provide legal advice?** No. Diwan provides general guidance and draft templates only.  
**Are government schemes verified?** No scheme records are currently published; the dataset awaits official-source and owner review.  
**Can I pay or top up credits?** No. Wallet actions are synthetic test simulations only.  
**Does it send documents to AI providers?** Not in the current mock staging configuration. Never use real personal documents in this preview.  
**Does it guarantee scam detection?** No. It highlights risk indicators and safe next steps; it cannot make a definitive fraud finding.

## Claims we must not publish

- “100% accurate”, “guaranteed fraud detection”, “official government partner”, “legal advice”, “instant approval”, “all schemes verified”, “secure for all sensitive documents”, or any unmeasured speed/uptime claim.
- Any live-payment, wallet-balance, account-deletion, hosted-auth, AI-provider or 120-FPS claim until independently verified.
- Any testimonial, user count, revenue, performance or outcome metric without auditable evidence.
