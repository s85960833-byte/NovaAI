## Writing rules
- Prefer short, everyday Hindi. Avoid dense Sanskritised language in primary buttons.
- Use one action per button and consistent verb forms.
- Keep legal/financial terms paired with a short explanation.
- Do not imply official government affiliation, guaranteed outcomes, guaranteed scam detection, or legal advice.
- Hindi body text uses a 1.6–1.7 line height, minimum 16 px, and no letter spacing.
- Product labels must be translated through the i18n dictionaries; do not hard-code visible strings in components.
